Questions & fine print

What happens to unused hours?
Up to 20% of your monthly hours roll over once. Beyond that they expire, because we reserve capacity for you either way.
Can we change plans?
Yes — move up at any time, effective immediately. Moving down takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
Do you charge for discovery?
The first call and a written scope summary are free. Longer research engagements are billed against your plan hours.
Who owns the code?
You do. Every repository, design file and credential is yours from the first commit, with no licence conditions attached.
How does billing work?
Invoiced on the first working day of each month, net 14, by bank transfer or card. Prices are in USD and exclude local sales tax.

Travel and third-party services (hosting, monitoring, paid APIs) are passed through at cost with receipts. Rates shown are valid for engagements starting in 2025.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us what you are building and we will suggest the smallest plan that gets it done. Contact us or read more about the studio.
