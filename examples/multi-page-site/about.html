About us

We are a small studio building calm, fast, no-nonsense software for teams who would rather ship than configure.

Our story

Northwind Studio started in 2016 as a two-person consultancy working out of a rented desk above a bakery. The first project was a scheduling tool for a regional courier company — a single page, one database table, and a lot of conversations with dispatchers about what actually slowed them down.

That project set the pattern we still follow: sit with the people doing the work, remove steps instead of adding screens, and ship something usable in weeks rather than quarters. Nine years later the team is eleven people across four time zones, and the bakery is still our unofficial test kitchen.

Today we design and build web products for logistics, healthcare admin and education. We keep our stack deliberately boring so the software we hand over can be maintained by the people who inherit it.

What we value

  • Clarity first

    If a feature needs a tutorial, it needs a redesign. We write plain interfaces and plain documentation.

  • Small, reversible steps

    We release often, measure honestly, and keep every change easy to undo.

  • Durable over clever

    We choose well-understood tools that will still make sense to a maintainer five years from now.

  • Say the real number

    Transparent estimates, transparent pricing, no surprise line items.
